What is JCR?

Just Competitive Racing (JCR) is a competitive F1 sim racing league built around the passion for fair, high-level motorsport competition. We bring together drivers from across the globe to compete in structured championship seasons.

Founded with the goal of creating a space where sim racers can test their skills against equally dedicated competitors, JCR has grown into a thriving community of passionate drivers.

League Structure

JCR is organized into three competitive tiers, ensuring drivers of all skill levels can find the right challenge:

  • Tier 1The top tier. The fastest and most experienced drivers battle it out for the ultimate championship title. Races are intense, margins are tight, and consistency is key.
  • Tier 2The proving ground. Drivers looking to step up from Tier 3 or push for Tier 1 promotion compete here. The racing is highly competitive with close championship battles.
  • Tier 3The entry point. New drivers and those developing their racecraft start here. It's the perfect place to learn, improve, and prepare for promotion to the higher tiers.

Each tier has 10 teams with 2 drivers per team, mirroring the real F1 grid. Promotion and relegation between tiers happens at the end of each season based on performance.

Race Format

Every race weekend follows a structured format designed to deliver the most competitive and realistic experience:

  • Qualifying — All drivers compete in a qualifying session to determine the grid order for the race. Lap times decide everything.
  • Feature Race — The main event. Full-distance races with strategy, tyre management, and wheel-to-wheel action. Points are awarded based on finishing position.
  • Sprint Races — Selected rounds include shorter sprint races for bonus points, adding an extra layer of excitement to the calendar.

Points are awarded for both drivers and constructors standings, just like real F1. The scoring system ensures every position matters from first to last.

Stewarding & Rules

Fair racing is at the core of JCR. Our dedicated FIA team reviews every incident to ensure consistent and fair stewarding across all tiers.

  • Incidents are reviewed using onboard and broadcast replays
  • Penalties follow a clear and transparent penalty system
  • Persistent rule-breaking may result in grid penalties or race bans
  • Drivers can appeal decisions through the proper channels
  • The stewarding team operates independently to ensure impartiality

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join JCR?

Head over to our Discord server and follow the sign-up process. New drivers are placed in a tier based on their pace during a trial.

What game is used?

JCR races are held on EA Sports F1 — the official Formula 1 game.

When are races held?

Race days are scheduled weekly.

What platform do you race on?

JCR is a cross-platform league — drivers can compete from PlayStation, Xbox, or PC.

Can I bring a friend?

Absolutely! Everyone is welcome. Your friend will go through the same sign-up and trial process to find the right tier for them.
